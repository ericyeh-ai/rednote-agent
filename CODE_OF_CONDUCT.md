# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone will be treated with respect and dignity, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical background.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** - Treat all community members with courtesy and respect
- **Being constructive** - Provide helpful feedback and constructive criticism
- **Being inclusive** - Welcome and support people of all backgrounds
- **Being patient** - Remember that we're all learners; no question is too basic
- **Being professional** - Keep discussions civil and on-topic

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

- Harassment based on protected characteristics (age, race, gender, etc.)
- Hate speech, slurs, or derogatory comments
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention
- Threats or violence
- Spam or off-topic behavior
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. If you witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to [your-email@example.com] or through GitHub's reporting tools.

## Consequences

- **Warning**: Private message explaining the violation and expectations
- **Temporary Ban**: Exclusion from community for a set period
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from the community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All community spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, etc.)
- Representation of the project in public spaces
- Official project events or communications

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, please open an issue or contact a maintainer.
